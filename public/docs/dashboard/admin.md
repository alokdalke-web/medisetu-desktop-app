# Admin Dashboard

![Admin dashboard — key metrics, revenue chart and live panels](/docs/images/dashboards/dashboard-admin.svg)


Your command center for clinic-wide operations. Everything you need to assess performance at a glance.

---

## Key Metrics (Top Cards)

| Metric | What It Shows | Healthy Sign |
|--------|---------------|-------------|
| **Total Revenue** | Revenue with % vs previous period | Steady or growing |
| **Total Appointments** | Booking count with trend | Consistent week-over-week |
| **Total Patients** | Patients registered | Growing month-over-month |
| **No Shows** | Patients who missed appointments | Below 10% of total |
| **Pending Payments** | Outstanding amounts not yet collected | Kept low |

---

## Revenue Overview Chart

Time-series chart with daily/weekly revenue and appointment count overlay.

**What to watch for:**
- Revenue dropping while appointments are steady → average revenue per visit is falling
- Sudden spikes or dips → investigate specific days for anomalies
- Weekday vs weekend patterns → adjust scheduling accordingly

Side-by-side comparison: Completed, Confirmed, Pending, Cancelled, No-Show.

## Top Symptoms

The most reported symptoms across recent consultations. Useful for:
- Identifying local outbreaks (sudden cluster of fever cases)
- Seasonal trends (allergies in spring, flu in winter)
- Service planning (high demand for specific consultations)

---

## Patient Overview

A snapshot of patient activity for the selected period — registrations and recent patient interactions for fast follow-up.

> For deeper trend charts — patient growth over time and no-show trends — use **Reports** (Patients and No-Show Analytics).

## Daily Admin Checklist

- [ ] Check earnings and appointment count for anomalies
- [ ] Look at no-show count — rising trend needs immediate action
- [ ] Monitor patient queue for growing delays
- [ ] Review pharmacy low-stock alerts
- [ ] Check notifications for errors or pending approvals
